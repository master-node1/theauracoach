# Personas

Assumed from the brief and the India-first default; validate with 6–8 interviews in Phase 2.

## Priya — the busy home exerciser (primary)

- 31, product manager in Bengaluru, vegetarian (eggetarian on weekends), lactose-sensitive.
- Goal: lose 6 kg, feel energetic. Trains at home with 2 adjustable dumbbells and a mat, 30–40 min, 3 days/week, early morning.
- Pain points: calorie apps don't know her mother's sambar; plans assume a gym; feels guilty after missing days.
- Needs: fast Indian food logging by katori/roti, short plans, kind reminders, protein ideas without meat.
- Success: logs 5 days/week, completes 2–3 sessions/week for 8 weeks.

## Arjun — the structured gym-goer

- 24, software engineer in Hyderabad, non-vegetarian, trains at a commercial gym 5 days/week.
- Goal: muscle gain and a 100 kg squat.
- Pain points: random programmes, no progression tracking, unsure about protein from Indian meals.
- Needs: progressive overload, RPE logging, PR charts, raw vs cooked chicken handled correctly, barcode scanning for whey.
- Success: hits weekly volume targets, sees e1RM rising.

## Meena — the cautious returner

- 46, school teacher in Pune, non-vegetarian, mild knee pain, hypothyroid (managed by her doctor).
- Goal: general fitness and mobility after years of inactivity.
- Pain points: fear of injury, overwhelmed by jargon.
- Needs: safety screening, low-impact plans, clear explanations, large text, a coach that tells her when to ask her doctor.
- Success: walks and trains 3×/week without pain for 12 weeks.

## Rahul — the content and safety admin (internal)

- Certified trainer and nutrition reviewer working for AuraCoach.
- Needs: fast content editing with preview, review queues for flagged AI answers, versioned prompts, audit trail.

## Anti-persona

People seeking treatment for eating disorders, clinical diets or rehabilitation. The product detects signals (very low targets, rapid-loss requests, purging language) and redirects to professional help.
